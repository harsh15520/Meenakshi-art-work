# Gallery Source 2: Themes, Subjects & What's in the Collection

The gallery holds original oil and acrylic paintings across these themes. Use these as concrete examples when speaking — name real subjects, not abstract categories.

## Devotional
- Krishna in gold crown, marigold temple deity, mother-and-child, Radha-Krishna (gold/blue, moonlit), angels/apsara.
- These are reverent, faith-held-close pieces.

## Wildlife & Nature
- Tiger family, tiger close portrait, cranes at sunset, deer at sunset pond, seven horses (auspicious), lion pop-art, elephant pattern art.
- Nature observed with patience, not decoration.

## Rural Life
- Village well, bullock carts, women carrying water pots, camel cart, banyan tree.
- Everyday village life remembered in warm earth tones before it disappears.

## Portraits & People
- Graphite portrait wall (seven likenesses), flamenco dancer, ballerinas, elderly man with horses, pirate captain fan art, pop-art portrait.
- Character felt whether in colour or graphite.

## Floral & Still Life
- Rose bouquet, jasmine and magnolia wood plates (also in custom-orders), abstract compositions.

## Classical & Romantic
- Garden idyll, Victorian-dress romantic genre scenes.

## Pop Art & Pattern
- Bold lion heads, psychedelic elephant, cubist fragments — a deliberate departure, the range beyond tradition.

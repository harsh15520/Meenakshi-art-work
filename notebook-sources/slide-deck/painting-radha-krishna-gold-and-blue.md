## Describe the slide deck you want to create

Create a short, detailed "how this painting was made" handout deck for the gallery painting **"Radha-Krishna in Gold & Blue — Blue, and the Gold That Follows"** (acrylic on canvas, 24 × 30 in). Use only the supplied source. Build slides in this order:

1. **Title slide** — "Radha-Krishna in Gold & Blue · Blue, and the Gold That Follows," Acrylic 24 × 30 in, gallery piece, one-line hook: *blue settling, gold answering, two figures who don't need to face each other to be together.*
2. **The temperature choice** — three references studied; the moonlit version was too cool and lost the earthly warmth the buyer wanted; the theatre duet was lovely but too loose for the centre wall; the chosen midday study is a warm blue that stays luminous rather than dark.
3. **The turn — gold first, blue second** — the Day 2 rule from the notebook: gold first, blue second, or the halo dies; the warm gold halo is laid first, then the luminous blue floats wet-on-wet over its edge so the two breathe; laying gold first is what keeps the blue alive.
4. **The palette** — Luminous blue ground (the night around them, mixed to glow not recede), Warm gold halo (devotion made visible, laid first), Rose on Radha (earthly warmth, keeps her human amid the divine blue).
5. **Where the gold catches** — morning east window: halo reads as pale fire, blue stays recessive; noon flat light: gold and blue reach equal weight, the most balanced read; evening lamp: the halo glows and the blue deepens into night.
6. **The detail most miss** — the flute at the side, resting not raised (music implied, the pause between notes); their near-touching hands (fingers almost meet but don't — the tension of restrained devotion, not union).
7. **Where it belongs / closing** — above a console where the evening lamp is lit before guests arrive, the gold catching them at the door; for someone who knows the lore, likes warmth over spectacle, wants a daily not a display — and a pointer to message on WhatsApp.

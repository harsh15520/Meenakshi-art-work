## Describe the slide deck you want to create

Create a short, detailed "how this painting was made" handout deck for the gallery painting **"Mother & Child — The Quiet Between Them"** (oil on canvas, 2 × 1.5 ft). Use only the supplied source. Build slides in this order:

1. **Title slide** — "Mother & Child · The Quiet Between Them," Oil 2 × 1.5 ft, gallery piece, one-line hook: *the small, wordless hush that passes between a mother and a child before either speaks.*
2. **The sweetness problem** — mother-and-child is one of the most painted subjects, and most of it leans saccharine (round cheeks, glowing halos, everything resolved); the risk was making something pretty that said nothing; the brief was a feeling the buyer remembered from childhood but couldn't name.
3. **The turn — lowering the gaze** — the mother's eyes rest down on the child, never meeting the viewer; that single choice turns a portrait into a private moment we only overhear; three studies tried eye contact and felt like greeting cards, the lowered gaze was the fourth and felt true.
4. **Warmth without heat** — soft ochre ground (lamplight temperature, not sunlight), muted rose on the child (a blush, never bright, so the child reads as held not highlighted), deep umber shadow (grounding, encloses the moment instead of staging it).
5. **The blessing hand (hidden symbolism)** — the mother's raised hand hovers near the child's head but doesn't touch — a blessing held in suspension; the child's fist clenched in the mother's dupatta is the only tense line, where love becomes physical.
6. **What people almost miss** — up close the blessing hand is barely painted, a few rose-grey strokes that resolve into a hand at a distance; the single hard line is the child's fist — tenderness becoming grip.
7. **Closing slide** — how it lives: hung low children point first, a bedroom night-light companion, guests go quiet then say "oh" — and a pointer to message on WhatsApp.

Keep all text short and phrase-level. English.

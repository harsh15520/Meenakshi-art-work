## Describe the slide deck you want to create

Create a short, detailed "how this painting was made" handout deck for the gallery painting **"Lion · Pop Art — The Lion, in Loud Colour"** (acrylic on canvas, 24 × 30 in). Use only the supplied source. Build slides in this order:

1. **Title slide** — "Lion · Pop Art · The Lion, in Loud Colour," Acrylic 24 × 30 in, gallery piece, one-line hook: *flat colour, hard edges, a stare from print not the wild.*
2. **The realism trap** — a lion almost has to be painted realistic (mane, fur, savannah); the brief wanted to break that reflex and treat the lion as a graphic object, not a nature study; the buyer wanted range on the wall.
3. **The turn — repetition as the mane** — the mane built from repeated stencil-like shapes, not drawn strokes; the pattern becomes the subject, the lion becomes a poster; free-drawn manes drifted to realism until a fixed repeated unit forced the graphic read.
4. **Four colours, no apology** — Signal orange (body, first eye-landing colour), Cobalt blue (negative space, pushes orange forward), Hot magenta (mane accents, third pop), Black outline (the hold, hard edges).
5. **Keeping it flat** — masked flat fills, no blending across boundaries (blending would betray the print); uniform outline weight on every edge (equal weight = poster, not sketch).
6. **Where it belongs** — young person's room, studio corner, entrance pop; for someone who wants the wall to speak first: bold, graphic, unapologetic.
7. **Closing slide** — studio line: *every painting eventually leaves the studio; the story it carries never does* — and a pointer to message on WhatsApp.

Keep all text short and phrase-level. English.

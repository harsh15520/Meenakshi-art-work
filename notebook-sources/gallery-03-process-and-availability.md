# Gallery Source 3: How a Gallery Painting Comes to Be & How to Buy

## The Making
- Every painting is hand-painted, never printed. Oil and acrylic only.
- A single piece can take six weeks of layering — e.g. "Evening Light at the Pond" was painted over six weeks, layer by layer, until the evening light finally felt right.
- Student pieces are also shown — a celebration of growing confidence and original expression.

## The Studio Pipeline (visible on the page)
When a painting is still in the studio, it moves through:
1. Finishing varnish
2. Drying
3. Framing
Then it is ready to leave the studio and become available.

## Availability
- The marquee is labelled AVAILABLE NOW — these are paintings you can purchase today.
- "Recently Added" shows the newest available piece.
- "In the Studio" shows a work not yet for sale — the visitor is invited to check back when it's framed.
- Prices range from ₹1,500 (small still life) to ₹20,000+ (large wildlife/pop).

## How to Enquire / Buy
- The page's WhatsApp message: Hello, I am interested in purchasing a painting. Please share currently available artwork and prices.
- The visitor messages on WhatsApp; Meenakshi replies personally, usually the same day, with current pieces, dimensions, pricing and delivery options.
- There is no online cart — every purchase begins with a conversation.

## Commission vs Purchase
- If the visitor sees a painting and thinks "that is exactly what I need" — they buy from the gallery.
- If they need something that doesn't exist yet — they go to /custom-orders.
- The gallery audio speaks to the first person: the one browsing, recognising, choosing.

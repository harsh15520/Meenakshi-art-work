## One-line concept

A single-page visual cheat-sheet for "how a carved wooden welcome gate gets made" — the brief, the carving-meets-colour problem, the material, the 5-week build, and the two details people miss.

## Sections to lay out

### 1. The commission at a glance (fact strip)
- **Subject:** Peacock Courtyard Gate (welcome gate)
- **Medium:** Carved Wood, sealed + layered acrylic on relief
- **Size:** 6 × 4 ft
- **For:** A private family home · **Completed in:** 5 weeks · **Delivered:** Saharanpur · **Price:** By enquiry

### 2. The brief
- A carved wooden gate for a private courtyard entrance, hand-painted with peacocks beneath a flowering tree.
- It had to greet guests before they stepped inside — say welcome the way the family actually welcomes.
- Client voice: *"with live birdsong and flowers."*

### 3. The craft problem
- A carved gate and a painted gate are two different crafts.
- Let carving do structure, paint do life, without one fighting the other.
- Clients wanted one object made by one hand — not a carved frame with a picture.

### 4. The material (callout)
- **Carved seasoned wood, sealed** — gives weight and shadow.
- **Layered acrylic on the relief** — holds bright peacock colour without cracking outdoors.
- Rejected: pure relief (lost colour), printed panel (defeated handmade), metal inlay (too cold).

### 5. The 5-week timeline (vertical strip)
- **W1** Carve arch + neem trunk → **W2–3** Peacocks in relief, carved then underpainted → **W4** Blossoms + sealing → **W5** Installation in Saharanpur, checked in morning light.

### 6. Two details people miss (callouts)
- **Relief peacock eyes** — carved a few mm proud, catch sunlight; most notice the shimmer, not the reason.
- **The neem is the frame** — its branches form the arch's outline; step back and the gate is the tree.

### 7. Living at the threshold
- Guests pause and photograph the gate before the house; neem leaves shift through the day; the family's "we're home" marker.

## Visual cues
- Tall 6 × 4 ft gate silhouette; a carved-arch motif, neem branches drawn as the arch outline.
- Peacock eyes shown with a small sun-ray mark to signal the proud relief.
- Two stacked craft icons: a chisel (carving = structure) and a brush (paint = life).
- A faint morning-sun gradient across the relief to show the moment the gate "read alive."

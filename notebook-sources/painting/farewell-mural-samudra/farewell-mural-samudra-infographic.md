# The Churning of the Ocean — Infographic Spec · "The Grand Gesture"

> **Source type:** Commissioned mural infographic (one-page cheat-sheet) · **Slug:** `farewell-mural-samudra`
> **Painting:** The Churning of the Ocean · Acrylic Mural, 8 × 6 ft · **Layout:** landscape (wide wall) preferred
> **Companion source:** `farewell-mural-samudra-curator-report.md`

## One-line concept

A single-page visual cheat-sheet for "how a monumental farewell mural gets made" — the brief, the scale problem, the two client requests, the 8-week build, and the wall's transformation.

## Sections to lay out

### 1. The commission at a glance (fact strip)
- **Subject:** The Churning of the Ocean (Samudra Manthan)
- **Medium:** Acrylic Mural
- **Size:** 8 × 6 ft
- **Occasion:** Farewell gift · **Completed in:** 8 weeks · **Space:** Institutional hall

### 2. The brief
- A farewell mural for a mentor who gave everything.
- It had to feel monumental — the effort, not just the achievement.
- Client voice: *"remind future generations of what persistence looks like."*

### 3. The scale challenge
- Canvas-read ≠ wall-read.
- Figures must read from across a large hall **and** hold detail up close.
- Solved with: multiple scale studies + viewing-angle tests from many points in the hall.

### 4. Two requests that shaped it (callouts)
- **Vasuki wraps the whole composition** — the serpent is the backbone of the story, not a side detail.
- **Treasures emerge gradually** — achievement as a process, not a moment; some treasures still forming.

### 5. The 8-week timeline (horizontal strip)
- **W1** Brief & philosophy → **W2** Wall prep & surface test → **W3–4** Scale & figure studies → **W5–6** Colour layering (mural acrylics) → **W7** Detail (Vasuki, treasures) → **W8** Installation on ceremony day.

### 6. The transformation
- **Before:** a blank, functional dividing wall.
- **After:** a teacher — students pause, faculty teach perseverance, a landmark for directions.

### 7. Living in the hall
- Students research the story; faculty use it to discuss collective effort; it anchors the institution's wayfinding.

## Visual cues
- Wide 8 × 6 ft frame motif; a faint before/after split suggesting the slider interaction.
- Vasuki drawn as a wrapping spine across the composition sketch.
- Treasure icons in staged opacity (some solid, some ghosted) to show emergence.

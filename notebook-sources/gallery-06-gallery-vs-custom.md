# Gallery Source 6: Gallery vs Custom-Orders — Same Studio, Two Doors

This source keeps the two page audios from blurring into each other.

## Gallery Page (this audio)
- Hero: Art that changes the feeling of a room.
- Visitor mindset: I am browsing. I want to see what exists and maybe take one home.
- Action: look at the collection, recognise something, message to buy.
- Emotional register: appreciation, recognition, warmth, a little wonder.
- Language to use: available now, original, hand-painted, the feeling of a room, catches the light, part of your home.

## Custom-Orders Page (separate audio, already written)
- Hero: Made for one person. Remembered for years.
- Visitor mindset: I have a story/person/memory and I want it made real.
- Action: share the story, receive a one-of-a-kind painting.
- Emotional register: intimacy, trust, memory, legacy.

## The Key Distinction
- Gallery art is found. You see it, it speaks to you, you take it home.
- Commissioned art is made for you — from your story.
- The gallery audio must NOT invite the visitor to "tell me your story" or "commission." It invites them to look, recognise, and enquire about what is already here.

## When a Visitor Chooses Gallery
- They see a painting and think: That is exactly what I need.
- They want to know price, size, and how to bring it home.
- The audio ends by pointing them to WhatsApp: message me about the piece you love — I'll share sizes, pricing and delivery.

## What should the video focus on?

Create a calm, grounded cinematic walkthrough of the gallery painting **"Village Well · Banyan — The Well Everyone Knew"** (acrylic, 2 × 3 ft) that feels like a remembered village, not a postcard. Open on the idea that this painting is about a routine, not a place — the walk to the well, the wait in the banyan's shade. Explain the central problem it refused: the bright flat 'colourful India' postcard, and the buyer who grew up at such a well and wanted its weight, not a brochure. Make the dry-brush root technique the visual star — show the banyan's aerial roots built with a nearly-empty brush, hundreds of separate dry strokes, so the tree reads solid from far and fragile threads up close, exactly how real roots hang. Reveal the earth palette (terracotta soil, dusty olive banyan, thin pale sky) and why it stays earth, not rainbow. Touch on the banyan as the village's meeting place and the well as its first daily errand, then show the details most miss — the roots dissolving into strokes close, and the chipped second pot kept uncorrected. End on where it lives: a home far from the village, a quiet daily reminder in soft morning light, with the studio's "story it carries never does" line. Carry an ambient sound bed throughout — pulley creak, wind in the branches, sparrows at dusk.

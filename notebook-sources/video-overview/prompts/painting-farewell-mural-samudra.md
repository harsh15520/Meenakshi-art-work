## What should the video focus on?

Create an immersive, cinematic walkthrough of the commissioned mural **"The Churning of the Ocean — The Grand Gesture"** (acrylic mural, 8 × 6 ft, institutional hall). Open with the idea that some farewells are too large for words and needed a wall — mythology that speaks about effort itself, so future generations see what persistence looks like. Weave in the client's own words (monumental, holding the whole story). Explain the central technical problem — canvas-read does not survive wall scale, so figures had to read from across a hall yet keep detail up close, solved through scale studies and viewing-angle tests. Spotlight the two client requests that shaped the composition: Vasuki wrapping the entire work as its backbone, and treasures emerging gradually so achievement reads as process not moment. Compress the eight-week build into a time-lapse (philosophy → wall prep → studies → layering in mural acrylics → ceremony-day final strokes). End on the transformed hall — students pausing, faculty teaching perseverance, the wall as a landmark — with the studio's "story it carries never does" line. Keep it monumental and unhurried.

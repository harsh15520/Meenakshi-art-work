## What should the video focus on?

Create an immersive, sensory cinematic walkthrough of the gallery painting **"Tiger Family — The Quiet Hour Before Sunset"** (acrylic, 24 × 30 in). Lead with the hook that this painting captures what the tigers *feel* — the breath-held hour before sunset — rather than the usual aggressive tiger cliché. Explain the central move: the mother's gaze directed into the distance, not at viewer or cubs, which turns the whole work into calm vigilance rather than confrontation, and how several eye studies led there. Walk through the three-colour palette (warm gold, cool shadow blue, deep earth) and the hidden symbolism (mother protective between viewer and cubs; cub half-hidden, safety vs curiosity). Make the *light* the visual spine: sweep the canvas from flat 4 PM, to golden-hour glow at 6 PM, to deep protective shadow at 7 PM. Use the ambient sound bed (wind, distant birds, leaves) and close on the golden-hour hero with the studio's "story it carries never does" line. Keep it cinematic and unhurried — this is a painting about stillness, not spectacle.

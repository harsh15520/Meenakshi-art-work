## What should the video focus on?

Create an immersive, cinematic walkthrough of the commissioned painting **"Shravanabelagola · Ruins & Monolith"** (acrylic, 2 × 3 ft, painted for a son, for his father). Open with the idea that some commissions are of a place and this one was of a memory — a hill an old man climbed every year as a boy, painted small and carried into his study so the climb could continue in conversation. Weave in the client's own words (bringing the hill back into his study, and into their conversations). Explain the central tension — the brief asked for the monolith "small, like a memory," the opposite of a hero-shot, so a famous landmark (the colossal Bahubali) had to feel intimate and remembered, never monumental. Show the composition turn: the hero composition rejected as too postcard, the intimate one chosen with the monolith small among the ruins, and keep the resting step as the personal landmark. Let the light be the visual spine — dawn (silhouette against first light), midday (flat and honest climb), dusk (stone warms, memory closest) — tracing the climb from first light to closest memory. End on the study: the painting above the bookshelf where the father reads, conversations starting with "look at the painting," and the studio's "reads larger the longer you live with it — as memories do" line. Keep it quiet, warm, and unhurried.

## What should the video focus on?

Create a quiet, cinematic walkthrough of the gallery painting **"Mother & Child — The Quiet Between Them"** (oil, 2 × 1.5 ft) that feels like a held breath rather than a presentation. Open on the idea that this painting holds a feeling — the wordless hush between a mother and a child before either speaks. Explain the central risk it refused: the mother-and-child cliché of round cheeks, glowing halos, everything resolved, and how the brief was instead a childhood feeling the buyer couldn't name. Show the key move — the lowered gaze, where the mother's eyes rest on the child and never meet the viewer, turning a portrait into a private moment we only overhear (three greeting-card studies, then the true fourth). Reveal the lamplight palette — soft ochre ground, muted rose on the child, deep umber shadow — and the suspended blessing hand that hovers near the head but never touches, with the child's fist in the dupatta as the one tense line. Highlight what people miss: the blurred blessing hand that resolves only at distance, and the single hard edge of the child's grip. Let the edit breathe through the light-simulator spine — cool morning window light, full afternoon warmth where the rose blooms, lamp-lit evening where the ground glows and the moment encloses — and end on the studio's "story it carries never does" line. Keep the pacing slow, tender, and unhurried, matching the painting's own quiet.

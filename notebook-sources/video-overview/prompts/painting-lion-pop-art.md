## What should the video focus on?

Create a punchy, cinematic walkthrough of the gallery painting **"Lion · Pop Art — The Lion, in Loud Colour"** (acrylic, 24 × 30 in) that feels like a poster coming to life. Open on the idea that this painting declares a lion rather than observing one — flat colour, hard edges, a stare from print not the wild. Explain the central break: the realism trap (mane, fur, savannah) that the brief deliberately refused, treating the lion as a graphic object for range on the wall. Show the key move — the mane built from repeated stencil-like shapes so the pattern itself becomes the subject and the lion becomes a poster, and how free-drawn manes kept drifting to realism until a fixed repeated unit locked the graphic read. Reveal the four-colour palette (Signal orange, Cobalt blue, Hot magenta, Black outline) and the flat technique: masked flat fills with no blending, and a uniform outline weight that makes it a poster not a sketch. End on where it belongs — a young room, studio corner, or pop entrance — with the studio's "story it carries never does" line. Keep the edit sharp and graphic, matching the painting's own hard edges.

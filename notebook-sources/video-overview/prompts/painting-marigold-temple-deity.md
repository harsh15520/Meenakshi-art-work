## What should the video focus on?

Create an immersive, cinematic walkthrough of the commissioned devotional painting **"Marigold Temple Deity"** (acrylic, 2 × 1.5 ft, part of a home puja corner set of three). Open in the quiet of dawn at the corner — a diya lit, the soft ring of the temple bell, distant morning chanting — establishing the ambient sound bed that defines this sacred space. Weave in the client's own words (the puja corner finally feels finished, the diya lit every morning, the paintings catching the light first thing). Explain the central problem: a set of three devotional works can feel like three separate commissions, so the goal was to compose them as one corner — related, not repeated. Spotlight the two client requests that shaped the composition: the morning diya light hitting the paintings first thing (the gold composed to catch the early flame), and Radha-Krishna facing the entrance to greet anyone who comes in. Make the continuous gold line the visual spine of the video — trace it slowly across all three paintings so the corner reads as one devotion — and let the diya's light meet that gold at its 6am angle, warming the marigold at the deity's feet. Close on the full corner in morning light, the mother-and-child blessing as the focal point of daily prayer, with the studio's "a corner finished is a home at peace" line. Keep it warm, unhurried, and devotional.

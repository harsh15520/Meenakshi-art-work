## What should the video focus on?

Create an immersive, cinematic walkthrough of the commissioned table set **"Made for the Table — Flowers She Can Eat Off"** (hand-painted wood; Jasmine plate, round, 16 in; plates and a tray). Open with the idea that some commissions are for the wall and this one was for the table — sent from a daughter to her mother, meant to be used and washed, not stored and admired. Lead with the client's own words: *"I sent these to my mother for her birthday. She feeds us all on them now — the flowers match the jasmine in her garden."* Make the two visual stars the hand-painted florals and the invisible finish: hold long on individual jasmine petals, on the soft magnolia pink of the companion plate, and on the leaf-green scrollwork tracing the rim so the bloom reads as sitting in a garden rather than floating — then show the food-safe sealant as three clear passes that change nothing you can see, followed by water and daily washing running over the same petal with the flower still intact. Explain the central problem plainly: painted tableware usually fails one way, too precious to use or too plain to love, and this brief demanded both, because the mother would use them daily and a display-only gift would have been a quiet insult. Cover the material reasoning — acrylic on wood under a food-safe sealant, with ceramic, untreated wood and printed melamine set aside — and the daughter's two non-negotiables: her mother's actual garden jasmine, not a generic flower, and a tray safe for real food. Compress the four-week build into a time-lapse (wood prep and base → hand-painted jasmine and magnolia → food-safe top coat → dispatch to Pune for the birthday), with the notebook lines about matching the garden photo and three coats of sealant. End on the set in daily service — the mother serving the family on them, grandchildren recognising the jasmine as "hers," the tray that became the birthday-table centre piece and stayed — with the studio's "story it carries never does" line. Keep it warm, close-up and unhurried.

3. The Village Room — village life, wells, bullock carts. Note: "remembered in warm earth tones before it disappears."
4. The Wildlife Room — tigers, cranes. Note: "nature observed with patience."
5. The Portrait Wall — seven graphite likenesses. Note: "character doesn't need colour to be felt."
6. The Pop Room — bold, graphic, modern. Note: "a deliberate departure, the range beyond tradition."

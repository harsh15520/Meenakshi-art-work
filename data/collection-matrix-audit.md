# Collection Matrix — Stage 1 Audit

Full visual audit of every painting across the two source folders that feed the Gallery and Custom Orders pages, tagged across eight curatorial dimensions. This is the raw input for Stage 2 (naming the actual exhibition collections) — nothing here is wired into the site yet.

**Scope:** 39 paintings total — 23 in `public/images/painting/` (Gallery source) + 16 in `public/images/custom/` (Custom Orders source).

**Note on "Scale":** every value in that column is a *visual estimate only*, inferred from framing, canvas edges, and surface detail visible in the photo. No real physical dimensions were measured or available — treat these as rough impressions, not fact, until confirmed against actual inventory.

**Verification:** 7 entries were spot-checked directly against the source images (5.jpg, 6.jpg, 51.jpg, 112.jpg from `painting/`; 2.jpg, 31.jpg, 56.jpg from `custom/`) — all matched the tagging below closely.

## Groupings that surfaced during the audit (raw signal for Stage 2, not final)

**From the Gallery folder:**
- **Pop-art/psychedelic multicolour set** — 5, 24 (lions), 82 (elephant), 12 (portrait): bold graphic language, would visually clash if mixed with the traditional devotional/rural works.
- **Devotional core** — 20, 40, 80, 93: Krishna/Radha, symbolic/devotional, warm gold palettes.
- **Rural/village narrative set** — 112, 116, 122 (and 40 as a devotional-tinged variant): large-format documentary genre scenes.
- **Wildlife realism set** — 6, 50, 51, 66, 113: naturalistic animal subjects, distinct from the pop-art lion/elephant pieces.
- **Smaller/lighter accent pieces** (salon-wall candidates, not standalone) — 49 (floral), 69 (folk portrait), 101 (abstract).

**From the Custom Orders folder:**
- **Wood-object trio** — 2, 41, 81: small floral pieces painted directly on wood (plates/tray) — a natural "functional decor" set, best clustered rather than hung as standalone centrepieces.
- **Pencil portrait cluster** — 7, 106, 107, 109, 125, 158, 160, 26: eight graphite portraits/figure studies of varying finish; 106/109/125 are the most polished (could stand alone), 107/158 read more as studies.
- **Devotional/mythological trio** — 31, 56, custom-work-1: strong colour, large-format, symbolic — centrepiece candidates.
- **Decorative statement pieces** — 23, and secondarily 158/160: vivid, folk-art/ornamental standalone décor pieces.
- **Sole landscape/heritage piece** — 162: unique in this set, wide horizontal format.

**Cross-folder observation worth flagging for Stage 2:** the devotional/mythological subject (Krishna, Radha, deities) appears strongly in *both* folders (Gallery: 20, 40, 80, 93; Custom: 31, 56, custom-work-1) — a "Sacred India" collection would likely need to pull from both source folders, not just one, if the collections are meant to represent the full catalogue rather than mirror the existing Gallery/Custom-Orders page split.

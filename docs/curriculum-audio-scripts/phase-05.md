## Phase 05 — Signature Style

### Intro
Welcome to the last studio door, friend — Phase 05, Signature Style. This is the longest stretch, ten weeks and more, and the most personal one we offer. Here, the teaching steps back and your voice steps forward.

### Focus
The theme is depth, realism, and a personal voice. You'll develop a personal subject and palette that feels unmistakably yours. Then series work — repeating one motif until your voice appears in the rhythm of it. And finally, presenting and sequencing a body of work, so your paintings speak together, not just alone.

### Tools
Now you choose. Your medium at full strength — oils or acrylics, used with real confidence. A portfolio case and a simple digital documentation setup, because you'll photograph every work. A variety of supports — canvas, board, paper — for different ideas. And framing and presentation materials, because the work deserves a finished edge.

### The Work
We begin with Choosing Your Subject — listing what genuinely pulls you, then picking one. Your Palette Statement turns favourites into identity. Series Planning maps five works around a single motif; the First Series Painting makes theory practice, and Variation Studies deepen your intent. A Personal Master Copy lets an artist you admire become a teacher. Then Portfolio Sequencing arranges your works for narrative flow, and Portfolio Presentation photographs, frames, and finishes the body of work.

### Outcome
You leave with a cohesive body of personal work and the confidence to keep developing your own visual language. No one tells you when you've found your style — it just starts showing up.

### Close
The references fade, and what remains is unmistakably yours. That quiet studio breath? That's you, painting what only you could have seen. Go gently, and keep going.

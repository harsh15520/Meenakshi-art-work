## Phase 01 — Hand Control

### Intro
Welcome, friend. Settle in, because this is where every painter's journey in our Saharanpur studio truly begins — not with a masterpiece, but with the simple, honest conversation between your hand and a pencil. I'm so glad you're here.

### Focus
Phase 01 is called Hand Control, and its whole promise is this: lines, forms, and confident movement. We start with the basics that most of us were never taught — how to actually hold a pencil. You'll try the tripod grip, the overhand grip, and the underhand grip, and learn when each one serves the kind of line you're after. Then we work on line weight, using pressure and speed to make a mark that's whisper-soft or grounded and sure. And we build pressure sensitivity — from the lightest hatching to a shadow line you can lean on.

### Tools
The tools here are quiet but essential. You'll meet a range of graphite pencils — HB, 2B, 4B, 6B — so tone can grow gradually instead of jumping. A kneaded eraser lifts graphite without hurting the paper's tooth. A3 cartridge paper, 120 to 160 gsm, is sturdy enough to survive all your shading. A sharpener and sandpaper block keep a long, controllable lead, and blending stumps feather your tone without ever using your fingers.

### The Work
The work is playful and freeing. We begin with First Lines — two minutes of looping scribbles that loosen the wrist. Then the Tripod Grip Drill and Overhand Grip for Shading teach control and speed. You'll build a Pressure Scale from whisper to press, then Hatching Bands and a Cross-Hatching Grid. We draw contours, then blind contours, then quick Gesture Poses. By the end you'll shade a Sphere Study and finish a Still-Life of Three Objects that brings every skill together.

### Outcome
Here's what you walk away with: by the end of Phase 1, you can draw any form from observation with a steady, intentional line — and your hand no longer fights the pencil. That's a gift you'll feel in every phase after this.

### Close
So take a breath, pick up that pencil, and trust the process. Your hand is learning to listen. See you in the next phase.

## Phase 04 — Oil Painting Basics

### Intro
Hello, and welcome to the studio's quiet room — Phase 04, Oil Painting Basics. If acrylic was fast and loud, oil is slow and honest. Take your time here. You've earned it.

### Focus
This phase takes you from concept to a complete canvas. You'll learn fat-over-lean layering and how to work with slow-drying mediums without cracking your work. Then colour temperature and edge control — the hard, soft, and lost edges that make a painting feel real. And finally, the full arc: blocking, refining, and resolving a painting from a blank canvas to a finished image.

### Tools
The tools are richer now. Oil paints — your primaries plus earth tones. Linseed oil and an odourless solvent, or a solvent-free medium if you prefer. Hog bristle brushes for laying paint, and sable or synthetic for finishing. A primed stretched canvas becomes your real support, and a palette, rag, and medium cups keep your setup clean and deliberate.

### The Work
We open by feeling the difference — Oil vs Acrylic — then learn Mediums & Fat-over-Lean so layers stay sound, and Solvent Safety & Setup to protect you both. You'll tone the canvas, lay an Imprimatura Wash, then Block-In Shapes and Establish Value. Colour Strings keep your hues consistent; an Alla Prima Study builds confidence. Then the magic of edges — Soft Edge, Hard Edge, and Lost & Found Edges. You'll paint Drapery, Metal, Glass, and Skin Tone Mixing, then a Portrait Block-In, a Landscape in Oil, and a Still-Life in Oil. Finally, Scumbling & Glazing, Detail & Resolution, Unifying the Whole, and your Signature & Varnish Prep lead to the Complete Canvas Project.

### Outcome
You walk away able to take a painting from a blank canvas to a resolved, lit, atmospheric image — in the slow, honest language of oil.

### Close
Stay with the paint long enough and it tells the truth. Rest now. One phase remains.

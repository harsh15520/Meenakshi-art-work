# Phase 04 — Oil Painting Basics

## Overview
Phase 4, "Oil Painting Basics," is an eight-week passage from concept to a complete, resolved canvas using the slow, honest language of oil paint. Across 28 exercises and 48 student examples, students learn fat-over-lean layering, edge and temperature control, and how to carry a painting from a blank support all the way to a finished, lit, atmospheric image.

## Focus Areas
- Fat-over-lean layering and working with slow-drying mediums
- Colour temperature and edge control (hard / soft / lost)
- From concept to finished canvas — blocking, refining, resolving

## Tools Introduced
- Oil paints (same primaries plus earth tones)
- Linseed oil / odourless solvent (or a solvent-free medium)
- Hog bristle and sable/synthetic brushes — stiff for laying, soft for finishing
- Primed stretched canvas — the real support for a finished work
- Palette, rag, and painting-medium cups — a clean, deliberate setup

## Exercises

1. **Oil vs Acrylic Feel**
   - Duration: 15 min
   - What to do: Compare a stroke in each medium side by side.
   - What you learn: A slow medium invites patience.
   - Success looks like: You feel the difference immediately.

2. **Mediums & Fat-over-Lean**
   - Duration: 20 min
   - What to do: Mix linseed-rich and solvent-thin layers in correct order.
   - What you learn: Lean under, fat over — no cracking.
   - Success looks like: Test layers stay sound.

3. **Solvent Safety & Setup**
   - Duration: 20 min
   - What to do: Set up a clean, ventilated oil-painting station.
   - What you learn: Setup protects the work and you.
   - Success looks like: A tidy, safe working corner.

4. **Brush Care for Oils**
   - Duration: 15 min
   - What to do: Clean and condition hog and sable brushes.
   - What you learn: Clean brushes last for years.
   - Success looks like: Brushes restored, no caked paint.

5. **Toning the Canvas**
   - Duration: 20 min
   - What to do: Lay a mid-tone ground across the canvas.
   - What you learn: A ground unifies everything.
   - Success looks like: An even, non-white starting field.

6. **Imprimatura Wash**
   - Duration: 20 min
   - What to do: Thin-wash a warm tone for atmosphere.
   - What you learn: Warm grounds glow underneath.
   - Success looks like: Subtle warmth across the canvas.

7. **Blocking-In Shapes**
   - Duration: 30 min
   - What to do: Map big masses with thin colour, ignoring detail.
   - What you learn: Block before you build.
   - Success looks like: Accurate large shapes placed.

8. **Establishing Value**
   - Duration: 30 min
   - What to do: Set light/dark relationships early in the block-in.
   - What you learn: Value order drives realism.
   - Success looks like: A strong value map from the start.

9. **Colour Strings**
   - Duration: 25 min
   - What to do: Mix a light-to-dark string of one hue.
   - What you learn: Strings keep colour consistent.
   - Success looks like: An even progression of a family.

10. **Alla Prima Study**
    - Duration: 45 min
    - What to do: Complete a small study in one wet session.
    - What you learn: Direct painting builds confidence.
    - Success looks like: A fresh, complete small work.

11. **Soft Edge Technique**
    - Duration: 20 min
    - What to do: Blend a hard edge into a soft one.
    - What you learn: Edges tell distance.
    - Success looks like: A seamless edge transition.

12. **Hard Edge Control**
    - Duration: 20 min
    - What to do: Keep crisp boundaries where forms meet light.
    - What you learn: Some edges must stay sharp.
    - Success looks like: Intentional, clean hard edges.

13. **Lost & Found Edges**
    - Duration: 25 min
    - What to do: Hide some edges into the background.
    - What you learn: Lost edges create focus.
    - Success looks like: The eye led by what remains.

14. **Colour Temperature in Light**
    - Duration: 25 min
    - What to do: Paint warm light and cool shadow on a form.
    - What you learn: Temperature models volume.
    - Success looks like: Form turns convincingly.

15. **Reflected Light**
    - Duration: 20 min
    - What to do: Add bounced light into shadow sides.
    - What you learn: Shadows aren't flat black.
    - Success looks like: Shadows feel alive.

16. **Cast Shadow Logic**
    - Duration: 20 min
    - What to do: Paint a shadow consistent with the light source.
    - What you learn: Shadows obey the light.
    - Success looks like: Shadows fall correctly.

17. **Painting Drapery**
    - Duration: 35 min
    - What to do: Render cloth with folds and value steps.
    - What you learn: Cloth is form in repetition.
    - Success looks like: Believable folded fabric.

18. **Painting Metal**
    - Duration: 30 min
    - What to do: Capture a metallic vessel's hard highlights.
    - What you learn: Metal needs extreme contrast.
    - Success looks like: Surface reads as metal.

19. **Painting Glass**
    - Duration: 30 min
    - What to do: Suggest transparency with edges and reflections.
    - What you learn: Glass is mostly what's behind it.
    - Success looks like: A convincing transparent vessel.

20. **Skin Tone Mixing**
    - Duration: 30 min
    - What to do: Mix a range of plausible flesh tones.
    - What you learn: Skin is many colours.
    - Success looks like: Natural, varied skin tones.

21. **Portrait Block-In**
    - Duration: 45 min
    - What to do: Block a portrait in oil from life or photo.
    - What you learn: Same plan, richer medium.
    - Success looks like: A solid portrait foundation.

22. **Landscape in Oil**
    - Duration: 1 class
    - What to do: Paint a landscape with atmospheric depth.
    - What you learn: Distance cools and lifts.
    - Success looks like: A deep, airy landscape.

23. **Still-Life in Oil**
    - Duration: 1 class
    - What to do: Build a lit still life with reflective objects.
    - What you learn: Oil rewards patience.
    - Success looks like: A rich, resolved still life.

24. **Scumbling & Glazing in Oil**
    - Duration: 30 min
    - What to do: Use slow glazes to deepen a passage.
    - What you learn: Oil glazes glow from within.
    - Success looks like: A luminous adjusted passage.

25. **Detail & Resolution**
    - Duration: 30 min
    - What to do: Bring one area to full finish selectively.
    - What you learn: Finish where the eye lands.
    - Success looks like: Focal area reads as complete.

26. **Unifying the Whole**
    - Duration: 25 min
    - What to do: Step back and harmonise colour across the canvas.
    - What you learn: The whole must agree.
    - Success looks like: A cohesive overall colour.

27. **Signature & Varnish Prep**
    - Duration: 20 min
    - What to do: Sign and learn when to varnish.
    - What you learn: A finish protects the work.
    - Success looks like: Signed and correctly prepped.

28. **Complete Canvas Project**
    - Duration: 2–3 classes
    - What to do: Take one painting from blank to resolved.
    - What you learn: The full arc becomes yours.
    - Success looks like: A finished, framed-ready oil.

## Outcome
You can take a painting from a blank canvas to a resolved, lit, atmospheric image using the slow, honest language of oil.

## Parent & Student Notes
- Oil teaches patience, and that is the point — drying is slow, so a painting evolves over several classes. There is no rush, and "staying with one thing" is the real skill being built here.
- Safety first at home: if she paints outside the studio, use an odourless solvent or a solvent-free medium and keep the area ventilated. Brush care after each session keeps good brushes usable for years.
- Watch for the moment she stops fighting the medium and starts letting it carry the colour — that shift, usually by the landscape or still-life class, signals real confidence.
- The two-to-three-class Complete Canvas Project is the capstone; encourage her to enjoy the long arc rather than race to "finished," since oil rewards the time spent resolving.

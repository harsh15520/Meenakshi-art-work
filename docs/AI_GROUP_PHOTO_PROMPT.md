# AI Group Photo — Prompt & Specs

> **Status:** Prompt ready. Image generation needs to be done externally (Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, etc.).

---

## Prompt

```
A warm oil-painting-style class portrait of 16 Indian art students and their teacher in a sunlit Saharanpur studio. The students — young women and men of various ages — stand and sit in a relaxed, joyful arrangement around a large wooden table covered with paint tubes, brushes, and half-finished canvases. The teacher, a woman in a cotton kurta, stands at the center with a gentle smile, holding a paintbrush. Behind them, the studio walls are lined with finished oil paintings in ornate frames. Golden afternoon light streams through a window, casting soft shadows and catching dust motes in the air. The mood is intimate, proud, and alive with creative energy. Rich warm tones — burnt sienna, cadmium yellow, ultramarine — against cream walls. Style: realistic oil painting with soft brushwork, reminiscent of Indian contemporary figurative art. No text, no logos.
```

## Composition Description

- **Layout:** Triangular arrangement — teacher centered, students radiating outward in 2–3 loose rows
- **Background:** Studio interior with paintings on walls, wooden table in foreground with art supplies
- **Lighting:** Golden hour from a side window, soft fill light from the opposite side
- **Mood:** Warm, intimate, proud — a family of artists, not a formal class photo

Hero image above the polaroid wall section, or as a print in the studio.
